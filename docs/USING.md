# Using Life OS

Life OS works on a rhythm. The two habits that matter most are the **daily plan and shutdown** and
the **weekly review**. Everything else exists to feed them.

## First ten minutes
Open **Setup** (Today shows a Start here card while the app is empty): your areas in your own words,
one goal per area, up to five habits, then a brain dump of everything else. Nothing from the brain
dump is saved until you approve it.

## A day
- **Morning, 5 minutes: Plan day.** Look at what's left over, pull tasks in from the Inbox, rank your
  **Top 3**, give each task an estimate, write one line of intention. If you planned the night before,
  tomorrow's Top 3 is already waiting.
- **During the day:** press **C** (or the + button) to capture anything in one line, for example
  `call mum tomorrow #REL ~20m` or `submit paper due 30 nov #WRK`. Add a quick journal line between tasks.
- **Evening, 3 minutes: Shutdown.** Sweep unfinished tasks (done, tomorrow, someday or drop), tick
  habits, pick tomorrow's Top 3, reflect briefly. Nothing rolls over on its own.

## A week
**Review → Weekly review**, about 25 minutes, ideally Friday: clear the Inbox, look at the real
numbers (planned vs done, deep work, hours per area against your targets, habits, mood), check goals,
write the reflection, set three outcomes for next week. A reminder arrives Friday evening.

## Longer cycles
Monthly (30 min), quarterly (rate each area 1 to 10, score key results, set the next quarter's
goals) and annual (walk the year, rewrite your standards). Trends appear after your first reviews.

## Quick capture syntax
| Write | Result |
|---|---|
| `#WRK #HLT #REL #FIN` | assigns the area |
| `~20m`, `~1h30`, `~2h` | estimate |
| `tomorrow`, `friday`, `20 dec` | schedules it for that day |
| `today` | puts it on today |
| `due 30 nov` | sets a deadline instead of a day |

## Shortcuts
`C` capture · `⌘K` search and jump · `1` to `6` screens · `I` inbox · `,` settings · `?` this list.
On a phone, tap a task's title to reveal its actions.

## With Claude Code
Once the MCP server is registered (see SETUP), you can say "add the tasks from this email to my
inbox", "what did I plan for Thursday", or "draft my weekly review from the data". Drafts land in
the review for you to edit.

## Principles the app follows
- One orange per screen means "look here".
- Habits are capped at five; missing once is fine, the app only nudges you not to miss twice.
- Reviews freeze the numbers as they were, so later edits don't rewrite history.
- Your data is yours: Settings exports everything as JSON or an Obsidian-style Markdown vault.
